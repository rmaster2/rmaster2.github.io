  • rclone-kyn은 소유자 본인의 Google Drive 파일을 소유자의 노트북에서 읽고 쓰는 용도로만 사용합니다.

English: rclone-kyn is used only by its owner to read and write the owner's own Google Drive files from the owner's laptop. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal or user data. Access tokens stay on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account settings.
